NanoNotes Get it

A PowerToys Command Palette extension

A note, kept before you lose it.

Type a thought, press Enter, and it’s saved. Search your notes and clipboard history together, then paste anything back into the app you came from. Plain files on your PC. No network, no accounts.

This sheet is live. Press Enter and your note joins the ledger below.

    Plain files, in your Documents

    Notes and clipboard history are JSON and PNG files you can open in any editor, back up however you like, and read without NanoNotes installed.

    Documents
    Documents/ └─ Notes/ ├─ notes.json ├─ clipboard.json └─ clipboard/ └─ 2026-01-15_10-15-00.png
    notes.json
    { "id": "note_001", "content": "call the landlord about the leak", "created": "2026-01-15T09:12:04Z", "lines": 1 }

    Clipboard history prunes itself after 7 to 30 days (14 by default, capped at 500 entries). Image capture is a switch.

    Nothing leaves this PC

    No sync, no telemetry, no accounts. NanoNotes makes zero network requests, so there is nothing to inspect.

    Install it

    Two ways. The Store keeps it up to date for you.

    Microsoft Store
    winget install NanoNotes

    Windows 10 (19041 or newer) or Windows 11, with PowerToys and Command Palette enabled. Press Alt+Space and type “notes”.
